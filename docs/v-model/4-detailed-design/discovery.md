# DD: discovery module of `mp305-core`

Status: approved (G4 discovery, user, 2026-10-01, revision 2; revision 3 approved 2026-10-01)

Refines AR-032 of [3-architecture.md](../3-architecture.md) (approved at
G3) and implements the `session::Connector` trait (DD-SESS-001) for real
devices, which the session's reconnection (AR-029) relies on. Device facts
cite [docs/research/device-model.md](../../research/device-model.md) with
their evidence labels. The module finds supplies over Bluetooth LE and USB
HID, decides everything decidable in pure functions (`classify.rs`: the
two classifiers, the sighting accumulator, the name choice, the identifier
shape test, the connect dispatch), and keeps the vendor calls in two glue
files (`ble.rs`, `hid.rs`) that only move OS data to and from the pure
part. The glue is proposed for the ADR-0013 class of exclusion through a
new ADR (section 5, decision 1). Coverage target 85 % for the module
without the glue (ADR-0008, "rest of `mp305-core`"). Revision 2 resolves
the independent review of revision 1 (LOGBOOK, "Store, csv and discovery
design reviews").

Rust module tree: `mp305_core::discovery` with `mod.rs` (`Found`,
`ScanOptions`, `Discovery`, the `Connector` impl, `not_found()`; no
`btleplug` or `hidapi` call, so IT-002's grep passes), `pub mod classify`
(pure, reachable from `tests/it_discovery.rs`), `ble.rs` (glue,
crate-private) and `hid.rs` (glue, crate-private). Every source file
starts with `//! Implements: DD-DISC-nnn`. No new dependencies. Timing
constants added to `protocol::timing` (AR-014 changed, section 5):
`SCAN_DEFAULT` 10 s, `SCAN_MIN` 1 s, `SCAN_MAX` 60 s (SR-002), `FIND` 4 s
(the scan that looks for one known identifier), `CONNECT` 10 s (the bound
on the OS connect, service discovery and MTU read).

## 4. Coverage exclusion

`ble.rs` and `hid.rs` hold only glue over `btleplug` and `hidapi` that a
device exercises: the event loop, `properties()`, `HidApi::new` and
`device_list()`. Everything decidable (DD-DISC-002, 003, 006) is in
`classify.rs` and unit tested. The two files are proposed for exclusion
through ADR-0014 (decision 1), which extends ADR-0013's class from the
transports to the discovery glue with the same rule (nothing testable may
live in an excluded file) and widens the pattern in AGENTS.md to
`(transport|discovery)/(ble|hid)\.rs`. Every function in them carries a
comment citing this section and the covering tests (IT-032 for the
classification they feed, ST-001 to ST-003 on hardware, UT-DISC-010 and
UT-DISC-011 by inspection).
