## Deviations and open points

1. UT-APP-006 (k) and UT-APP-028 (a) and (b) as approved in revision 3
   contradict the design items approved with them (DD-APP-020,
   DD-APP-021). The tests follow the design items: `DisconnectUnasked`
   from `Connecting` and `Lost`, and a close request in `Idle` that is
   simply not cancelled. The corrected entries are drafted as revision 4,
   approval pending.
2. UT-APP-027 (d) expected the mock to count a close after the session
   is dropped. Dropping a session calls no `close()` on the transport (a
   real transport disconnects in its `Drop`), so that part is not
   asserted; corrected in revision 4. In (a) the report's text is checked
   to start with `link lost: `.
3. The scan range was a number in `ui/connect.rs`; it is now the constant
   `model::SCAN_S_RANGE`, which the clamp uses too, and a test holds it
   equal to the core's `SCAN_MIN` and `SCAN_MAX`.
4. Three private widget helpers of `ui/screens.rs` (`field`, `button`,
   `rows`) do not have the form DD-APP-030 gives for drawing functions.
   Revision 4 states that the form applies to screens and screen parts.
5. UT-APP-011 and UT-APP-015 still pass with the first poll of DD-APP-003
   removed, because the task set polls new futures in push order. They
   hold the order of commands but not the first poll itself (TODO.md).
6. Additions beyond the design: screen and row labels and log texts in
   `texts.rs`, display helpers of the screens in `model.rs` (tested under
   UT-APP-023), a stop signal per session that ends the calls of a
   dropped session with one `Done` each (stated in revision 4), an extra
   case under UT-APP-012 (an exit during a reconnect's close).
7. Not verified here, and left for a session with a screen and the
   supply: the UT-APP-021 demonstration, UT-APP-024, the layout and
   drawing, Cmd+Q against the Dock's Quit, the Bluetooth permission
   prompt, and the Windows and Linux packaging. Only macOS arm64 was
   built.
8. DD-APP-021 as designed: while a prompt is open the close bound does
   not run. A worker that stops is now noticed on the next frame
   (`Model::worker_stopped`), which clears the prompt and the waiting
   close.
9. The decision between closing and dropping a session on an unasked
   disconnect reads the link state and then acts; a session that becomes
   ready between the two steps is the residual of that race.
